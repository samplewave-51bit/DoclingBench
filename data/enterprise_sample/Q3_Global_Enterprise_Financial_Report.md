# Q3 2024 Global Enterprise Holdings Financial & Operational Review
**Filing Reference:** SEC-10Q-Q3-2024  
**Reporting Period:** Quarter Ended September 30, 2024  
**Currency:** All dollar figures stated in USD Millions ($M)

---

## 1. Executive Financial Overview & Performance Summary
During the third quarter of fiscal year 2024, Global Enterprise Holdings achieved consolidated gross revenue of $4,850M, representing an 8.6% increase compared to $4,465M in the prior year period. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA expanded to $1,420M, yielding an EBITDA margin of 29.3%.
